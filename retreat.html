The Experience

Founder development, business transformation and holistic wellbeing — under one roof.

Four days at a private finca in Mallorca, intentionally structured to move between deep coaching work and genuine recovery. Each day carries its own focus — from arrival and grounding, to the founder as a person, to the leader and the business, to integration and a concrete plan for what comes next.

Pillar01

The Founder

Identity, values, energy and the patterns shaping how you lead.

Pillar02

The Leader & Business

Delegation, culture, team dynamics and the priorities that actually matter.

Pillar03

The Human

Movement, breathwork, time in nature and real, unhurried rest.

What you leave with

  • Your personal Founder Operating System
  • A 90-Day Transformation Plan
  • 3 personal, 3 leadership & 3 business priorities

Held by two coaches

Group coaching each morning, then parallel 1:1 walk-and-talk sessions with Marlene & Jakob — at the beach or the finca. Eight slots a day means every founder can book a session daily, with room to go deeper.

Practical Information

Dates, setting & investment

A private villa or finca in Mallorca, surrounded by nature — with space to move, think, connect and work, and enough distance from everyday life to see things differently. The final location is selected for access to nature, communal spaces, movement areas and quiet corners for reflection.

Who this is for

  • Founders who've built something meaningful — but rarely step outside it
  • Those navigating growth, change, or a new chapter
  • Founders who want their team to take more ownership
  • Those questioning what success should look like next
